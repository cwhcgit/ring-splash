# Ring Splash

A water ring toss game in the style of the classic handheld toy. Pump water
jets to blow rings up through the tank, tilt your phone to steer them, and hook
every ring onto a peg.

**Play in your browser:** https://cwhcgit.github.io/ring-splash/
(Best on an Android phone held sideways. Tilt needs the page opened directly
in Chrome, not inside another app's viewer.)

## Controls

- Tap the left or right side of the screen to pump that side: each press squirts one burst of water, and the pump refills when you let go
- Tilt the phone to steer the rings
- Keyboard: A / L to pump, arrow keys to tilt, Esc for the menu
